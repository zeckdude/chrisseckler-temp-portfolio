# Adim — company and product brief

Last updated: 2026-08-21. Mix of **public product truth** and **Chris’s memory** from case-study interview.

Chris: Senior Frontend Engineer, May 2023 – Mar 2024. Live: https://adim.io

---

## What Adim is (one breath)

Adim is a **creative collaboration platform for TV and movie production**. Chris’s surface: **design system**, Storybook, Tailwind, marketing site, **FE team process**.

---

## Before Chris (AD1 — E)

Fast startup, no real design system: duplicated components, visual drift, messy Tailwind, Storybook not team standard.

---

## What Chris built (AD2 — D + process)

- **Storybook** — UI standards and review before ship
- **Shared React components** — Next.js + TypeScript, in-repo library
- **Refined Tailwind** on top of components
- **Process:** PR template, regular **FE-only meetings**, proactive **future TD** identification

---

## Outcome (AD6 — E)

Consistency and speed **improved** for FE — fewer one-offs, better reviews, alignment. **No hard velocity metric.** ~10 months → **foundation** (system + process) that outlasts individual features.
